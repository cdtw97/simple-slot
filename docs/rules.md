# Fruit Arcade rules

A normal Free Play spin costs 5.00 virtual credits. Every board has three rows and six reels. A horizontal match pays once at its maximal length, starting at any reel. Each of the eight three-cell diagonals and the group of four corners may also pay. Overlapping patterns add. Each award is rounded half up to hundredths of a credit after its multipliers, then the awards are summed.

On a final normal board, each corner touched by an award lights once. Completing all four clears the lamps and queues three free spins at double payouts. Bonus spins leave lamps and normal spin counts unchanged. The triggering rules stay in effect until the earned bonus finishes.
